Who we are

We handle the parts
events break on

88 Events is an execution and logistics company based in Cairo. We get called in when an event has too many moving parts for one internal team to track — and when getting it wrong isn't an option.

From the first vendor call to final teardown, we own the execution side. Our clients show up. We handle the rest.

Primary target: Corporate Events & Summits
Secondary target: Product Launches & Brand Activations

Vision

The go-to events company in Egypt

To be Egypt's leading events company for execution and logistics — known for getting the details right on the biggest events in the region.

Mission

Take the logistics problem off your plate

We own the complexity of your event from planning through execution. What's left for the client is an event that runs on time, on spec, and gives nobody a reason to complain.

What we do

Core services

Corporate events

NGO & social impact events
